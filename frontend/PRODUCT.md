# Product

register: product

## Users
Sustainability and finance analysts at companies that report emissions. They upload activity data (energy, fuel, transport) exported from
other systems and need to know quickly whether it was accepted and what must be fixed.

## Product purpose
Turn a CSV of activity records into validated, stored data without waiting on the upload: see the state of every file, the rows that were
rejected with their line and reason, and the clean rows.

## Tone
Calm, precise, trustworthy. Plain words. Numbers first. No marketing language.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS dashboards with four identical gradient stat cards; glassmorphism everywhere; neon on black; stock photography; purple gradients.

## Strategic principles
1. State of the work is visible within seconds.
2. Never invent a number; say which period or sample a figure covers.
3. Errors tell the user what happened and what to do.
4. Accessible by default: contrast, keyboard, reduced motion.
